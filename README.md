# Company Case Studies

Independent analyses of real companies using publicly available data.
Each case asks one business question and answers it with a different toolset.

| # | Case | Question | Stack |
|---|------|----------|-------|
| 1 | UPI apps | Who is gaining as NPCI's 30% market-share cap deadline approaches? | Power Query, Power BI, DAX |
| 2 | Blinkit vs Instamart | Is quick commerce profitable yet? | Advanced Excel, Tableau |
| 3 | Google Merchandise Store | Which traffic sources bring buyers? | BigQuery SQL, Looker Studio |
| 4 | Airbnb | Do multi-listing hosts price differently? | Python, scikit-learn, NLP |

All analyses are independent and use public data. Not affiliated with
or endorsed by any company mentioned.
